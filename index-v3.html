About me

Crafting quality carpentry across Norfolk since 2003.

“I don’t sub-contract, I don’t cut corners — every project gets my full attention from start to finish.”

I’m a sole trader with over 20 years of hands-on experience in bespoke joinery and fitted furniture. Based in Norwich, I work across Norfolk and the surrounding areas — taking on projects of all sizes.

Every piece I make is built to last. Quality materials, traditional techniques and modern precision to deliver work that adds real value to your home.
