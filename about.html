About me

Crafting quality carpentry across Norfolk since 2003.

R. Plane Carpenter has been quietly building beautiful things across Norfolk since 2003. I measure, I make, I fit. The same pair of hands from the first conversation to the final coat of wax — no sub-contractors, no strangers in your home.

I work with homeowners and interior designers who care about the details most people never notice. The kind of people who know the difference between furniture that’s bought and furniture that’s made.

Every project starts with a conversation. I’ll visit your home, understand your space, and give you an honest quote. No pressure, no upselling — just quality carpentry, done right.
