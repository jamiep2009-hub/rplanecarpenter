# Your website editor — a quick guide

You can now update your own website: add photos, change any wording, add
reviews and update your phone number. No technical knowledge needed.

---

## Getting in

Go to **rplanecarpenter.co.uk/admin/** — you're already signed in.

**Put it on your phone's home screen** so it's one tap away:

- **iPhone:** open the link in Safari → tap the Share button → *Add to Home Screen*
- **Android:** open in Chrome → tap the ⋮ menu → *Add to Home screen*

It then opens like a normal app.

---

## The golden rule

Nothing you do goes live until you tap **Publish**.

Make as many changes as you like, look them over, and publish when you're
happy. If you close the editor without publishing, nothing changes on
your website.

After publishing, the website takes **about a minute** to update.

---

## Adding a photo

1. Tap **Gallery photos**
2. Tap **Add photo** at the bottom
3. Tap **Choose a photo** and pick one from your camera roll
4. Wait a couple of seconds while it's prepared
5. Fill in:
   - **Title** — what it is, e.g. *White shaker kitchen, walnut worktop*
   - **Short label** — the small gold text, e.g. *Country Kitchen*
   - **Which filter** — so it shows under the right tab on your gallery
   - **Description** — leave blank and one is written for you
6. Tap **Add photo**, then **Publish**

Photos are rotated, resized and compressed automatically. You don't need
to do anything to them first — straight off your phone is fine.

### Getting good photos

- Turn lights on and open blinds; daylight is best
- Stand in a corner to get more of the room in
- Take a couple from different spots and pick the best
- Wipe surfaces and move tools out of shot

## Homepage photos

**Homepage photos** is the grid on your front page. It works the same way,
but keep it to your **best 10 or so** — it's the first thing people see.

You can also choose how big each one appears: *Large*, *Wide* or
*Standard*. Mixing the sizes is what gives the grid its shape.

---

## Before & After

These are the sliders people drag to see a room change.

1. Tap **Before & After** → **Add a before & after**
2. Choose the **before** photo (the room when you started)
3. Choose the **after** photo (finished)
4. Add a room label (e.g. *Utility Room*) and a title
5. **Publish**

**The trick:** take both photos standing in roughly the same spot. The
slider looks far better when the two line up.

---

## Reordering and removing

Every photo and review has buttons:

- **↑ / ↓** — move it up or down the list
- **Edit** — change its wording or swap the photo
- **Bin icon** — remove it

The **first** review in your list is the one shown when someone lands on
your homepage, so put your best one at the top.

---

## Changing wording

Tap **Page wording**. Everything is grouped by page — tap a group to open
it. Type over any wording and tap **Publish**.

Two things worth knowing:

- Big headings are split into **two boxes**. The second line is the one
  that shows in italic gold.
- To make words **bold**, put two stars around them:
  `**like this**`

---

## Your phone number and email

Tap **Contact details**.

Changing your phone number here updates it **everywhere** on the website
at once — the contact page, the footer, and the WhatsApp button. Same for
your email.

---

## Made a mistake?

**Before publishing:** just close the editor. Nothing was saved.

**After publishing:** go to the main menu and tap **Undo last change**.
That puts the website back exactly as it was. You can undo an undo too,
so there's no way to get stuck.

Every single change is recorded permanently, so nothing is ever really
lost.

---

## Checking before you publish

Tap **Preview** on the bar at the bottom. That shows you the real page
with your changes on it, before anyone else can see it. Close the preview
and keep editing, or publish straight from there.

---

## Common questions

**Can I break the website?**
No. The editor can only change the specific wording and photos it shows
you — it can't touch the design, the layout or anything else. And if you
don't like a change, Undo puts it straight back.

**Do I need to do anything to my photos first?**
No. Take them on your phone and upload them as they are.

**Why hasn't my change appeared?**
Give it a minute, then refresh the page. If you're on the site already,
pull down to refresh.

**It's asking me for an "access key".**
That happens if your phone cleared its browsing data. Get in touch with
whoever set this up — it takes a minute to put back.

**Does it cost anything to run?**
No. There's nothing to subscribe to and no bill.

---

## Something not right?

If a change won't save, or something looks wrong on the website, take a
screenshot and send it on. Don't worry about breaking anything — you
can't, and everything can be put back.
